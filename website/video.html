Everything is a terminal command.

No settings UI. No side app. No second window to babysit.

batteries included

One terminal. Thirty-one plugins.

Every one is a plugin.toml — pure data. Nothing compiles, nothing slows your prompt, and @plugin disable <name> turns any of them off.
